Our story

Lux is Latin
for light.

Modern Asian fusion cuisine, served from a curated menu of fresh, bold ingredients, alongside an extensive selection of craft cocktails. A room designed for those who appreciate a high-end dining environment, without compromising on authentic taste.

LUX opened in mid-August 2026 in the Kettlestone Lakes development, off I-80 and Grand Prairie Parkway. It is the work of three Vietnamese friends who call themselves the LTJ Group, and the cooking is Vietnamese-forward, with Korean and Japanese accents across the menu. In the kitchen is Chef Raelynn Brockman, who previously cooked at Harbinger, HomeGrown and Cafe Trellis.

The lounge is built around a backlit bar and half-moon booths, and the dining room has the energy of a downtown hot spot.
